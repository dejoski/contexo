    How to play

    Contexo hides one secret word. Guess words to find it.

    • Ranks are clues. Every guess gets a rank: #1 is the secret itself, lower numbers are closer in meaning. A guess that isn't near the secret shows 500+.
    • The radar is your map. Each ranked guess lands on the radar as a colored dot. The arrow on every row points from your guess toward the secret — follow the arrows and the dots will converge on it.
    • Signal strength. The closer a guess is, the higher the signal (0–100). Gray dots on the rim are guesses that landed far off the map.
    • Colors match closeness: 🟡 #1 is gold, green is near, then lime, yellow, orange, red, and gray for far-off guesses.
    • Hints are unlimited. Tap 💡 Hint any time for a word about half as close as your best guess so far. Hints count in your score.
    • Daily vs Practice. Daily gives you one puzzle a day and builds a streak. Practice picks a random past puzzle — no streak, no stats.
    • Stuck? Give up reveals the word and the full top-150, but your streak breaks.
    • Challenge a friend copies a link to today's puzzle so you can both play and compare share cards.

    Keep guessing until the ⭐ appears. Good luck.
